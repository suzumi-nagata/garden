Welcome, travelers, friends, and curious spirits, to Zumi's Grimoire.

![A closed SRS Grimoire on a candlelit desk, with a potion, a mouse mug, a black cat, glasses, dice and a trans-flag bookmark](../images/srs-grimoire.png)

As some of you may know, I am preparing for my long waited SRS (Sex Reassignment Surgery)
and, instead of treating this solely as a clinical procedure, I am approaching it as what
it truly is: a powerful act of becoming. I wanted a space to document my thoughts, track
my healing, and keep you all updated without having to cast the same spell (or send the
same message) a dozen times.

To make this diary easy to scan, every entry will start with a quick "Alchemist's Log"
header so you can gauge my status at a glance. Here is how to read the runes (some runes
may not appear on a given entry):

- **[ 📜 Entry ]** The entry number and title
- **[ 🌙 Phase ]** Which phase of the journey this entry belongs to (e.g., Gathering the Reagents)
- **[ 🔮 Subject ]** A short subject line for the entry
- **[ ⏳ Timeline ]** Where I am in the journey (e.g., Pre-Op Day -10, or Day +3)
- **[ 🧭 Alignment ]** My current mood, weather, moon phase, or what I'm waiting on
- **[ 🔋 Mana Level ]** My current energy and emotional state
- **[ 🩹 Vessel Status ]** Physical updates, pain levels, and healing progress

Feel free to read along, send good wards, or just lurk in the shadows as the journey unfolds. The circle is officially open.

_Yours in blooming,_ \
Zumi

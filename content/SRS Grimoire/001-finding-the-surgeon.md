> **[ 📜 Entry ]** 001
>
> **[ 🌙 Phase ]** Seeking the Oracle
>
> **[ 🔮 Subject ]** The quest for the true Healer
>
> **[ ⏳ Timeline ]** Day -?? (somewhere between when I was born and 2025-03-21)
>
> **[ 🧭 Alignment ]** Desperately dysphoric
>
> **[ 🔋 Mana Level ]** 40 / 100

Long before I officially transitioned (or even before I actually knew what a transgender
person was), I always felt the discomfort of being born on a "male's" body.

Even before puberty, I really hated my body, always felt ugly, felt liked I would prefer
to use my mom's/girls clothes, have a long hair, do girly stuff, but at the same time, it
felt... wrong, it felt like I was in the wrong for feeling this way.

Society made us think this was wrong to think (and the stereotypes on the 2000-2010s
didn't help either). Despite the ~~(what I now know it is called)~~ dysphoria, I tried to
hide that from everyone and buried that feeling down as much as I could.

Despite feeling wrong, at the time I knew that "some kind of surgery existed", but I was
really afraid of my parents at the time discovering, so I did the _"incognito while nobody
else was at home"_ research about the _"surgery to change sex"_ (or some kind of
combinations of that in Portuguese) that a curious pre-adolescence would do.

> [!info] Side note
>
> At that time, PCs were not as commonly available as today where everyone have their one
> machine. It was mostly common to have a family computer on the living room that everyone
> shared (which was my case).
>
> So researching that kind of stuff was really _"a risky move"_ to be done with someone
> else on the house.

I don't recall exactly what the first result url was, but I clearly remember the page. It
was a super plain html page, on a white background and had a lot of medical illustrations
(made with pencil) and explicit photos of the surgery. These images are ingrained into my
brain since then (2010s?, not sure exactly, but it was at least more than 10 years ago). I
dreamt of having that surgery one day, my mind kinda rolled a movie on the step-by-step of
the procedure and having it on myself. All of that, in secret of course, I was afraid of
anyone judging me for wanting that procedure done.

For a while, I secretly wished to magically wake up as a girl, I even tried to make a
[[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_thousand_origami_cranes|thousand paper cranes]] as I
heard that it could grant a wish, but I didn't have enough paper to complete it 🙁 (ended
up donating the ones I made before completing).

Going forward some years, the Thai surgeons always had their fame on the procedure. At
some point, I did thought of saving money to do the procedure in Thailand.

By the start of the covid pandemic, was when I was about to graduate from my bachelors and
started to make plans on finally start my transition (waited all my life for that 😢). It
actually took a few years before I felt it would be financially stable to start transition
in a way it wouldn't be risky to have to stop in the middle (which was something I really
think it was important as a go all in).

During the pandemic, one huge surgeon name that started to appear frequently was
Littleton, with the unusual case of doing jejunum as a primary surgery. After seeing some
first results, I was really astonished by how good they were. Obviously, I set an
objective to try to save money for that beauty ✨.

However, as time passes, I started my transition, a lot of health problems started to
appear (unrelated to HRT), some life issues that made it hard to save money in a
considerable form, and more importantly, the prices for the surgery exponentially
increasing.

Around 2024, when I was looking for surgeon options, I heard from a few doctors that
performed the surgery all covered by insurance (that is, without having to process the
insurance plan as I didn't want to have that headache). One name that called my attention
was Westin, that not only I saw some girls managing to do the surgery fully covered, but
also that he started to do the jejunum as a primary.

I then just put the idea aside for some months as there wasn't that much information
available online and I didn't want to get frustrated for another failed attempt. But by
march of 2025, I just ended up deciding to follow my dream and send an email to Westin's
team, which I'll talk more about on other notes.

Well I think I pretty much summarized a more than a decade on this quest, and that's
enough for a first single note.

_Until the next casting,_ \
Zumi
